## Relevance and area expansion

Default walking insights include recent public-space reports, excluding transport, campus, private-space, traffic and historical records. Locality search boxes are broad research scopes; they are not administrative boundaries, dangerous zones or incident geometry. A route intersecting a search scope can show a clearly labeled area reference.

If there is no relevant local-area report, the search expands to research scopes within 2 km of the route. The distance is measured to the scope boundary, never to a guessed incident center. Up to five recent references are selected, with three initially visible. Original reports and location limits open on tap. History is optional in About the data. Missing reports never establish safety.

## Facility map

`environment/walking.v1.json` extends the existing Salt Lake OSM extract with public police/hospital tags across the Greater Kolkata download box (22.35–22.80 N, 88.15–88.60 E). The raw query and hash are recorded in `environment/manifest.json`. The source is OpenStreetMap under ODbL, with original object links and edit dates. Contributor metadata and private addresses are omitted.

The merged audit accepts 9,146 road objects, 647 places and 1,143 areas. Raw merged counts include 48 police and 403 hospital tags; loader deduplication can reduce place counts. These are community map locations/building centers, with unconfirmed entrances and assistance. They are not an official complete station/hospital inventory. Street-condition geometry and lighting facts remain limited to the original Salt Lake extract; its collection date is preserved.

Routes receive up to four police and four hospital candidates within 800 m of route geometry. The map draws at most two of each, hides symbols below zoom 12, and avoids marker overlap. Map distance does not establish walking access or opening hours.

The [Kolkata Police station directory](https://kolkatapolice.gov.in/know-police-station/) is a useful official contact source, but it does not provide the independently located city incident feed needed to certify street safety. Whole-city completeness still requires a licensed official/partner feed and measured location validation.
