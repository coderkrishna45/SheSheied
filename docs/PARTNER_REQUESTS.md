**Subject:** Request for a small deidentified street-location pilot in Salt Lake Sectors II and V

Dear Officer / Data Steward,

We are developing SheShield, an Android walking companion for Salt Lake. We want to show attributable information and explicitly identify missing evidence. We do not want to generate crime pins from police-station addresses or imply that an unreported street is safe.

Could you advise whether a small deidentified extract of reported public-space incidents over the last twelve months can be supplied for approved research/demo use, initially around Sectors II and V? A CSV or GeoJSON export is sufficient; we do not require access to police systems.

Requested fields are a nonidentifying stable event ID, broad incident category and reporting status, occurrence date or date interval, public/private/transport setting, the actual incident street section or coordinate with stated uncertainty and road level, source/update reference, and subsequent corrections or withdrawals. Please exclude victim/suspect identities, residential addresses and case narratives. Where privacy requires coarse locations, please preserve that uncertainty; we will show area context rather than create street pins.

Please also describe collection boundaries, categories and time periods included, geocoding method, total records and unresolved/missing-location counts, update frequency, limitations, permitted app/demo reuse and attribution, retention terms, and a correction contact. A provider reviewer who can independently validate a small held-out location sample would help us measure wrong-street assignments. We will not infer completeness or accuracy from the existence of an export.

If this cannot be shared, an explicit description of what public deidentified material is available and whom to approach would be useful. We can provide the exact schema and prototype before any exchange.

Regards,
[Requester name, affiliation and reply contact]
